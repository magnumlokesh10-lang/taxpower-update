Keep these in mind

Monthly dates every filer should know

Standard dates for regular monthly filers. Extensions are notified separately, so check the official portal.

Full calendar
7of every month

TDS and TCS deposit

Deposit tax deducted or collected in the previous month. March is the exception, with a later date.

11of every month

GSTR-1

Outward supplies for the previous month, for monthly filers.

20of every month

GSTR-3B

Summary return and tax payment for the previous month, for monthly filers.
