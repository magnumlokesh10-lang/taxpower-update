Checklist

Before filing

Before GSTR-3B

  • Compare GSTR-2B with your purchase books
  • Check ITC you plan to claim, and what is blocked
  • Match tax liability with GSTR-1
  • Pay tax by challan, then file and keep the receipt
Read the full ITC checklist

Before a TDS return

  • Verify every deductee PAN
  • Match challan details with deductions
  • Validate the file with the FVU utility
  • Check late deduction and late deposit before filing
