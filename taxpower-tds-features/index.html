Questions

Questions about TaxPower TDS

Answers to questions that customers ask most often.

Which TDS forms can TaxPower TDS prepare?

It prepares 24Q, 26Q, 27Q and 27EQ returns, and the matching Form 138, 140, 144 and 143 under the Income-tax Act 2025, along with correction returns for them.

Can I file correction returns?

Yes. Request the consolidated file and justification report from TRACES, and the correction statement can then be prepared from them.

Can I bring deductee data from Excel?

Yes. Deductee, employee and challan details can be imported from Excel or text files, and the PAN list can be validated in bulk or one PAN at a time.

Can I download challans instead of typing them?

Yes. Challans can be downloaded from the income-tax portal and TRACES, book-entry details fetched with the BIN, or challans created from your own deduction entries.

Does it generate Form 16 and 16A?

Yes. Form 16 is prepared for salaried employees, Form 16A comes from TRACES, and Form 27D covers tax collected at source. You can sign, print and email them.

Can I file the return from inside the software?

Yes. It runs the file validation utility, creates Form 27A and files with OTP verification. The acknowledgement and RRR numbers are saved with the return.
