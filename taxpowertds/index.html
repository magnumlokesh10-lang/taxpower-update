Returns & forms

Which TDS and TCS statements can TaxPower TDS handle?

Every quarterly statement, the certificates that follow it and the corrections that sometimes come after.

  • 24Q · Form 138Salary deductions for each quarter, with the annual salary details filled in the last one. The new Act form replaces it from the new year.
  • 26Q · Form 140Tax deducted on payments to residents, with deductee, section and challan details linked line by line.
  • 27Q · Form 144Payments to non-residents, prepared the same way as the others, with the extra remittance details.
  • 27EQ · Form 143Tax collected at source, with collectee entries tied to the challans that paid it.
  • FVU and Form 27AValidate the file inside TaxPower, fix errors and regenerate. Form 27A is made for you.
  • Form 16 and 16ASalary certificates from your own data, other certificates from TRACES. Sign them and send by email.
  • Form 27DThe certificate for tax collected, ready to print, save as PDF or mail.
  • Correction returnsRequest the consolidated file, review defaults and prepare the correction without re-keying.

Daily work

Beyond the return: what a TDS desk does all quarter

Deducting, paying, checking and certifying all happen in one place.

  • File from inside the software

    Validate, create Form 27A and file with OTP, then keep the acknowledgement and RRR numbers with the return.

  • Work with TRACES

    Request consolidated files and justification reports, download certificates and read intimations without leaving the screen.

  • Keep challans in step

    Download challans from the income-tax portal, TRACES and NSDL, or create them from your deductions, then map them automatically and see the unused balance.

  • Clean PANs before filing

    Check one PAN quickly or the whole list in bulk, flag missing PAN and validate TDS and lower-deduction certificates.

  • See defaults early

    Short deduction, interest, late fee and mismatches show up before you submit, with a breakup by deductee and section.

  • Send certificates

    Sign Form 16, 16A and 27D, preview them and email each person straight from TaxPower.

  • Bring data in fast

    Load deductees, employees and challans from Excel or text files instead of typing them again.

  • Reports for review

    Registers, deductee-wise and section-wise statements and compliance reports, exportable to Excel and PDF.

Also included: backup and restore, role-based users, multi-user access over a network and updates inside the software.

Questions

Before you book a demo

Quick answers to what people ask first.

Which TDS forms can TaxPower TDS prepare?

It prepares 24Q, 26Q, 27Q and 27EQ returns, and the matching Form 138, 140, 144 and 143 under the Income-tax Act 2025, along with correction returns for them.

Can I file correction returns?

Yes. Request the consolidated file and justification report from TRACES, and TaxPower helps you prepare the correction from them.

Can I bring deductee data from Excel?

Yes. Deductee, employee and challan details can be imported from Excel or text files, and the PAN list can be verified together.

Does it generate Form 16 and 16A?

Yes. Form 16 is prepared for salaried employees, Form 16A comes from TRACES, and Form 27D covers tax collected at source. You can sign, print and email them.

Can I file the return from inside the software?

Yes. It runs the file validation utility, creates Form 27A and files with OTP verification. The acknowledgement and RRR numbers are saved with the return.
