What you can try

  • TaxPower GST: prepare and upload GST returns, download GSTR-1, 2A and 2B data, reconcile ITC and track filing status
  • TaxPower GST Billing: invoices, e-invoices, e-way bills, masters and reports
  • TaxPower TDS: 24Q, 26Q, 27Q and 27EQ returns (Forms 138, 140, 144 and 143), PAN and FVU validation, challans, TRACES and Form 16/16A certificates

How the trial works

  1. Request the trial from the form. Tell us which product you want to try and the kind of work you do.
  2. Check the system requirements page so that your computer is ready.
  3. We call you, help with installation and show you how to start. Help is available in Marathi, Hindi or English.
  4. Use TaxPower for 30 days. Call or message us if you get stuck.
  5. If it suits your work, choose an edition and buy. Prices are on the pricing page.

Trial terms such as the number of GSTINs or TANs that can be used are confirmed when you request the trial.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to questions that customers ask most often.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. TaxPower GST, Billing and TDS each have a 30-day free trial.

Which product should I try first?

CAs and tax consultants usually start with TaxPower GST or TDS. Businesses that raise many invoices often start with GST Billing. Tell us your work and we will suggest a starting point.

Do I need to be in Vidarbha?

No. Siddharth Enterprises is authorized to sell TaxPower across Vidarbha and also sells across India. The trial and training are online.

What happens after 30 days?

You can buy an edition, ask for guidance or stop using the software. See the pricing page for installation and renewal prices.
