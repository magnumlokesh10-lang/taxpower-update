Before you choose

TaxPower pricing questions

What is included, when AMC applies and how to pick an edition.

What does the installation price include?

The displayed price is the one-time installation fee. Support and software updates are free for the first year from purchase. 18% GST will be added to the displayed installation price. Optional LAN access is extra.

When does annual AMC apply?

AMC applies after the first year. The annual AMC for your selected products is shown separately from the installation price.

Which edition should I choose?

Professional is intended for CAs, consultants and tax practices; Business is intended for companies and finance teams. Installation and AMC vary by edition. Contact us to confirm the licence that fits your requirements.

Can I use TaxPower on additional LAN computers?

Optional LAN access costs ₹500 plus 18% GST per additional computer for Professional, or ₹1,000 plus 18% GST for Business. These charges are not included in the displayed installation price.

How will I receive my product key?

Your product key is delivered by email after confirmed payment. You can book a free demo or request a quotation before purchasing.

How pricing works

Payment terms

Please read these notes before you open the plan builder.

  • One-time installation fee

    The price shown for a plan is paid once, at the time the software is installed.

  • First year included

    Support and software updates are provided free for the first year after purchase.

  • Annual renewal

    From the second year, an annual renewal is required to continue support and updates. The renewal amount is shown beside the price in the plan builder.

  • GST is added on top

    GST at 18% is charged on the displayed installation price, so the invoice total is higher than the price shown.

  • LAN access is extra

    Use on more than one computer in the same office network is optional and is charged separately for each additional PC.

  • Combined modules

    Select GST, billing and TDS together in the plan builder to see one price for the modules you choose.

Choosing an edition

Professional or Business

Select the edition that matches how you use the software.

  • Professional

    Suitable for chartered accountants, tax consultants and practices that file returns for several clients and need client-wise records.

  • Business

    Suitable for companies and accounts teams that prepare returns for their own registrations.

  • Need help choosing

    Share the GSTINs and TANs you handle and we will recommend an edition during a demo.
